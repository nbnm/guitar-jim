## Design Context

### Users
Beginner and returning acoustic guitarists practising alone at a Mac, as defined in the supplied PRD. The screen must be readable from a seated playing position, with hands occupied by the instrument.

### Brand Personality
Arcade, rhythmic, encouraging. On 28 October 2026 the user replaced the initial warm notebook direction with a Guitar Hero inspired arcade direction.

### Aesthetic Direction
A dark arcade cabinet: charcoal stage, amber titles, five color-coded chord lanes, descending strum gems and a clear strike line. Futura Condensed for display and Avenir Next for reading. Several anchored lyric lines remain visible alongside the note highway. Audible metronome and countdown by default, per the user’s latest request. Synchronize countdown beeps, a pulsing ring, sound bars and a GO cue to the audio timeline. Provide visible mute and volume controls. No synthetic success sounds.

The app mark is a muscular cartoon Jimmy Page portrait with broad shoulders and visible biceps, dark curls, a sleeveless dragon vest, cream highlights and an amber spotlight, requested by the user. Use the bundled raster illustration for sidebar branding and the derived ICNS for the native app icon.

### Design Principles
- Give the current chord, phrase, beat and capture state priority.
- Keep recording and practice transport separate and explicit.
- Pair every feedback color with a label and symbol.
- Use generous type and keyboard access for hands-busy practice.
- Show honest empty and uncalibrated states, with no fabricated scores.
- Color and direction symbols identify musical events. The user's arcade request explicitly calls for a multicolor game surface.
- Give independent feedback for chord agreement, attack timing and microphone quality.
- Keep the current strumming pattern visible beside the lyric/chord panel. Show eight labelled subdivisions with down/up/rest; highlight the current subdivision from the practice clock. Offer an optional tabular chord-and-strum chart below the stage, with the current bar and subdivision highlighted. Preserve several visible lyric lines when the table is open.
